## Findings

### F-001 — `usage_events` retention explicitly unbounded (documented gap)

- Source: checklist/data
- Severity: Low
- File: `services/api/app/models/ingestion.py:22-27` (class docstring on `UsageEvent`)
- Issue: `usage_events` has no retention/archival policy — DATA-DESIGN.md § 4, PRD R-001, NFR-014. Individual-level activity (Confidential class) accumulates indefinitely. Not novel to this review — accepted as R-08 (LOW) in `PLAN.md § 6` and recorded in `DECISIONS.md`.
- Fix: track in a future retention/archival story; not remediable inside BED-01 by product decision (2026-08-26).
- Rule: security-baseline.md "Data" category (retention discipline for confidential data). Under the `standard` profile no rule *mandates* a bounded retention — this is `Low` and non-blocking; under a future `gdpr`/`hipaa` overlay this would flip to `High` compliance-tagged and require carry-forward resolution before merging that overlay in.
- Override: n/a — documented via docstring, not a `SECURITY-OK` suppression.

### F-002 — Encryption-at-rest for PII/confidential columns deferred to Postgres-level

- Source: checklist/data
- Severity: Low
- File: `docs/features/BED-01/DATA-DESIGN.md` § 4 (per-column Class table) — no code change requested.
- Issue: `ingest_tokens.user_email`, `user_roles.email`, `program_members.user_id`/`name`, `session_series.member_id`, `user_sessions.user_id` are all classified PII / Confidential (individual) per PRD §9.2. PRD explicitly delegates encryption to "standard org practice" at the Postgres level rather than defining a column-encryption mechanism in this story.
- Fix: acceptable under the `standard` governance profile; would require a `pgcrypto`/`pgcrypto`-backed column-encryption approach (or an application-layer envelope) if a `hipaa`/`gdpr` overlay is later activated. Track as a rule-drift item to revisit when a compliance overlay is added to `harness.yaml`.
- Rule: security-baseline.md § Data "Sensitive fields encrypted at rest with documented key management". Documented, satisfies the "documented" clause under `standard`.
- Override: n/a.
